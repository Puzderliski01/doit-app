Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

DoIT ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DoIT mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our service.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account via email or Google Sign-In.
  • Task Data: Tasks, categories, deadlines, and related data you create within the app.
  • Fitness Data: Workout logs, body measurements, and nutrition entries you voluntarily enter.
  • Usage Data: App interaction patterns, preferences (theme, language), and device information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the service, including cloud synchronization across your devices.
  • To send task reminders via email (only if you configure email reminders).
  • To provide AI-powered features (task breakdown, daily briefings) using Google Gemini API.
  • To improve the app experience and fix bugs.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Firebase (Google Cloud). We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your task data is private and only accessible by you. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Google Gemini API: Task titles and descriptions are sent to Google's Gemini API for AI features (task breakdown, daily briefings). This data is not stored by Google beyond the API call.
  • EmailJS: Email reminders are sent through EmailJS. Your email address is used solely for sending reminders you configure.
  • Firebase: Data is stored on Google Cloud Firestore servers.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app's Settings page.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all data through Settings > Delete Account.
  • Export: You can export your task data through Settings > Export Data.
  • Correction: You can edit any of your data at any time within the app.

GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
